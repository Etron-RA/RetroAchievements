## Game Site

## Overview
Help Luke Skywalker rescue Han Solo and Chewbacca from the Empire just in time for Life Day! Inspired by the infamous 1978 TV special, this action platformer brings Super Star Wars gameplay straight to the Sega Genesis. Slash through Imperial troops with your trusty lightsaber, survive platforming hazards, and get everyone home for the holiday reunion.
